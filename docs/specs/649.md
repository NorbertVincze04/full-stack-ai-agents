## Summary
On the booking page `/`, a click on a calendar day or a desk selects it, and a second click on the same day or desk deselects it (toggle). Today both only select: clicking the selected item again changes nothing.

## Scope
- `CalendarComponent`: clicking the selected day emits a deselect.
- `DeskPlanComponent`: clicking the selected desk emits a deselect.
- `BookingComponent`: starts with no day selected; handles "no date selected" (top bar text, neutral plan, disabled `Book Now`) and "no desk selected"; the own booked day and own desk are toggleable.
- Frontend specs for the toggle; update `docs/context/frontend.md` at build end.
- AC10 (Enter/Space, real browser) is verified manually by the human, not by an automated runner (D9).

## Functional requirements
1. Page load: no day is selected (no preselected first bookable day); the 4 bookable weekdays are still enabled.
2. Calendar: clicking an enabled unselected day selects it; clicking the selected day deselects it (also when it is a day the user has booked).
3. Desk plan: clicking an available desk selects it; clicking the selected desk deselects it back to `available`. The user's own desk (`yours`) is clickable and toggles the same way (D3).
4. Calendar and plan keep emitting events only; the selection state stays in `BookingComponent`.
5. Selecting another day resets the desk selection and reloads availability (unchanged); deselecting a day also resets the desk selection and clears availability (no request).
6. No day selected: top bar shows `No date selected`; all desks are `neutral` (no state, not selectable, signed in or not); `Book Now` is disabled (no click, no request, no message).
7. `aria-pressed` on day and desk buttons reflects the toggle state; a deselected item has `aria-pressed="false"`.
8. Disabled days, other users' booked desks and signed-out/neutral desks stay non-clickable (unchanged).
9. Keyboard: Enter/Space on a focused day or desk toggles it (native buttons).
10. Empty-desk state with a day selected is unchanged (`Please select a desk first`); signed-out `Book Now` with a day selected is unchanged (`You must log in first`).

## UI behaviour
- Click an unselected day or desk: it is highlighted (selected). Click it again: highlight removed.
- Load and after deselecting the day: no day highlighted, top bar `No date selected`, desks neutral and unselectable, `Book Now` neutral/disabled (not clickable).
- A day the user booked keeps its `booked` marker when it is deselected (marker is independent of selection).
- Own booked day selected: `Withdraw booking` as today; own desk shows `Yours`, and when clicked also a selected highlight (`aria-pressed="true"`); click again removes only the highlight.
- Deselecting the desk: plan shows it as available; `Book Now` shows `Please select a desk first`.
- Existing messages (`aria-live` area) are cleared on every selection change, as today.

## Manual verification of AC10 (human, real browser)
Setup: start the app (`cd Frontend`, `npm start`, open `http://localhost:4200`) or the compose stack; use Chrome. Sign in with a test user. Do not use real credentials in screenshots or notes.
1. Signed in, no day selected on load: press Tab until a bookable day button has focus (visible focus ring). Press Enter: the day becomes selected (highlight). Press Enter again: it is deselected (no highlight, top bar `No date selected`).
2. Same day: press Space to select, Space again to deselect. The page must not scroll on Space.
3. Select a day (click or key), Tab to an available desk. Press Enter: desk `Selected`; press Enter again: `Available`. Repeat with Space.
4. With the day selected and no desk selected, Tab to `Book Now` and press Enter/Space: `Please select a desk first`, no booking created.
5. Deselect the day: Tab through the page; `Book Now` must be skipped (not focusable). Click it with the mouse: nothing happens (no message, no request in the DevTools Network tab).
6. Optional with DevTools: each focused day and desk button shows an accessible name and `aria-pressed` switching `true`/`false` (Elements or Accessibility tab).
7. Pass: all of the above behave as described. Fail: note the step and browser; the Implementer fixes it and the check is repeated.
Acceptance: the human records "AC10 accepted" (for example in `docs/runs/649/e2e.md` or as a message to the Orchestrator); only then the Release Manager sets task 649 to `done`.

## Risk gate
- R1 (low, major functionality): today a date is always selected and the booking flow assumes it; allowing "no date selected" (after deselect) and a disabled `Book Now` changes that existing flow. The human chose this in Q1. Safer alternative: deselect only desks and keep the day selected (does not satisfy the task for days).
- R2 (low, major functionality): starting with no day selected (Q2) changes the initial state of the booking page; a user must click a day before booking, and existing specs assume a preselected day. Safer alternative: keep the first bookable day preselected on load. The human chose no preselection.
- R3 (low, UI only): making the own desk (`yours`) clickable adds a selection state on a desk that cannot be booked again; risk of the user mistaking a selection for a withdraw. Mitigation in AC7/AC8: toggling never calls the API and `Withdraw booking` stays the only withdraw action. Safer alternative: `yours` desk stays non-clickable (original Q3 option a).
- No security, database, container or pipeline items.
- R4 (resolved, human decision D9): a new e2e runner dev dependency (supply chain, lockfile used by CI and the image build). Resolved with the safer alternative: no runner; the human verifies AC10 manually.
- R5 (resolved, human decision D9): runner install and browser download behind the corporate proxy. Resolved with the same safer alternative: nothing is installed.
- R6, R7, R8 not applicable (e2e runner dropped); numbers not reused.

## Decisions
- D1 (Q1): No-date state is allowed: top bar `No date selected`, desks neutral and unselectable, `Book Now` neutral and disabled (not clickable); `Please select a date first` is not used.
- D2 (Q2): No day is selected on page load.
- D3 (Q3): Own booked day and own desk both toggle. "Toggle" means selection state only: clicking never creates or withdraws a booking and sends no request; `Withdraw booking` is the explicit action, is date-based (as today) and does not depend on the desk selection; the day's `booked` marker and the desk's `Yours` state persist while deselected.
- D4 (human, withdrawn by D9): proposal to add an e2e test runner so AC10 can be verified automatically.
- D5-D8 (withdrawn by D9): runner choice (Playwright on installed Chrome), mocked API, no CI, six journeys. ACs 12-20 are withdrawn / not applicable and were removed from the spec.
- D9 (human): no e2e runner will be added; R4 and R5 are resolved with the safer alternative. The human verifies AC10 manually in a real browser and accepts it; after that acceptance task 649 may be set to `done`. No dependency or pipeline change.
