## Summary
On the booking page `/`, clicking a calendar day or a desk selects it and clicking it again deselects it. No day is selected on load; without a day the top bar shows `No date selected`, desks are neutral and `Book Now` is disabled.

## What was implemented
### Frontend
- `CalendarComponent`: `dateDeselected` output when the selected day is clicked.
- `DeskPlanComponent`: `deskDeselected` output; own desk (`yours`) selectable with `pressed` flag, class `picked` and `aria-pressed`.
- `BookingComponent`: no preselected day, `selectedLabel` `No date selected`, `Book Now` `[disabled]="busy || !selected"`, `deselect()` / `deselectDesk()`, desk selection reset on day change, deselect and withdraw.
- Toggling never sends a request; `Withdraw booking` stays the only withdraw action.
- Details: [implement-frontend.md](../runs/649/implement-frontend.md).
